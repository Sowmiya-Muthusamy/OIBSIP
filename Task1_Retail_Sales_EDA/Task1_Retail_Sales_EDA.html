import matplotlib.pyplot as plt
import seaborn as sns

# Calculate total sales for each month
monthly_sales = df.groupby("Month")["Total Amount"].sum()

# Create the line chart
plt.figure(figsize=(12, 6))
sns.lineplot(
    x=monthly_sales.index.astype(str),
    y=monthly_sales.values,
    marker="o"
)

plt.title("Monthly Retail Sales Trend", fontsize=16)
plt.xlabel("Month")
plt.ylabel("Total Sales")
plt.xticks(rotation=45)
plt.grid(True, linestyle="--", alpha=0.5)
plt.tight_layout()
plt.show()
<h3 id="Observation">Observation¶</h3><p>Monthly sales fluctuated throughout the observed period. Sales reached their highest level in May 2023, while January 2024 recorded the lowest total. Since January 2024 contains very low sales compared with previous months, this result should be checked before making business decisions.</p>
